LIMITATIONS
A complete live match was not tested: the backend and original assets were
not uploaded. The original protobuf editor addon is needed for regeneration.

The original client has no complete decision screens for orderCards,
selectXValue, assignDamage, selectKnowledge, or pickCard. These are now
recognized, expose a decision_requested(kind, request) signal, preserve
available state, and explicitly pause with an explanatory message rather
than crash or auto-pass. Their decision screens and drafting remain unfinished;
this patch does not invent game rules or choose an answer on the player's behalf.
