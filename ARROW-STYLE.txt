TARGETING ARROW STYLE

The targeting and blocking indicators now use:
- Smooth cubic Bezier curves with a tapered, gradient stroke.
- A subtle wide glow, dark contrast outline, and fine highlight.
- Warm gold (#e5c180) for targets; soft blue (#80bddb) for blocks.
- A shaped, anti-aliased arrowhead aligned to the end tangent.
- Small travelling glints, an origin socket, and a restrained target reticle.
- Attachments just outside card/avatar edges to reduce overlap with card text.

Card movement and incremental updates are preserved. Existing arrow nodes are
reused; geometry is recalculated only when endpoints change. Animated accents
are a separate drawing layer above the stroke. These are Node2D-based visuals
and cannot intercept pointer input. Overlapping/very short arrows are suppressed.

src/GameBoard/TargetArrow.gd owns appearance and animation.
src/GameBoard/GameBoard.gd owns target IDs, colours and edge attachments.
The existing Line and Head child names are retained. Line.points now contains a
sampled curve, so use points[-1] for the endpoint rather than points[1].
